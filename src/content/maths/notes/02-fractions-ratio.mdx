# Fractions, ratio and proportion

<Key>
A **ratio** compares quantities. $3 : 5$ means "3 parts to 5 parts". A **fraction** is a part of a whole. They are two ways to describe the same relationship.
</Key>

## Fractions

- **Add / subtract**: use a common denominator. $\tfrac23 + \tfrac14 = \tfrac{8}{12} + \tfrac{3}{12} = \tfrac{11}{12}$.
- **Multiply**: multiply tops and bottoms. $\tfrac25 \times \tfrac34 = \tfrac{6}{20} = \tfrac{3}{10}$.
- **Divide**: multiply by the **reciprocal**. $\tfrac23 \div \tfrac45 = \tfrac23 \times \tfrac54 = \tfrac{10}{12} = \tfrac56$.
- **Mixed numbers**: convert to improper fractions first. $2\tfrac13 = \tfrac73$.

<Tip>
On your calculator use the fraction key, but show the method: common denominators or the reciprocal.
</Tip>

## Fractions, decimals and percentages

| Fraction | Decimal | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| $\tfrac12$ | 0.5 | 50% |
| $\tfrac14$ | 0.25 | 25% |
| $\tfrac15$ | 0.2 | 20% |
| $\tfrac18$ | 0.125 | 12.5% |
| $\tfrac13$ | $0.\dot{3}$ | $33.\dot{3}\%$ |

<Ext>
**Recurring decimal to fraction.** Let $x = 0.\dot{3}\dot{6}$. Then $100x = 36.\dot{3}\dot{6}$, so $99x = 36$ and $x = \tfrac{36}{99} = \tfrac{4}{11}$.
</Ext>

## Ratio

- **Simplify** by dividing by the highest common factor: $24 : 36 = 2 : 3$.
- **Share** in a ratio: add the parts, find one part, multiply.

Example: share 120 in the ratio $3 : 5$. Total parts $= 8$, one part $= 15$, so the shares are $45$ and $75$.

- **Unitary form** $1 : n$ is useful for scales. A map scale $1 : 50\,000$ means 1 cm on the map is 50 000 cm = 500 m on the ground.

## Direct and inverse proportion

- **Direct**: as one doubles the other doubles. $y = kx$. Cost of apples $\propto$ mass.
- **Inverse**: as one doubles the other halves. $y = \dfrac{k}{x}$. Workers and time: 4 workers take 6 days, so 8 workers take 3 days.

Find $k$ from one pair of values, write the equation, then substitute.

<Ext>
Other forms: $y \propto x^2$ means $y = kx^2$, and $y \propto \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x}}$ means $y = \dfrac{k}{\sqrt{x}}$.
</Ext>

## Rates

A **rate** compares quantities with different units: speed (km/h), density (g/cm³), price per kg.

$$\text{speed} = \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{time}}$$

**Best buy**: compare the **cost per unit** (price per gram or per litre).

## Worked example

$y$ is inversely proportional to $x$ and $y = 6$ when $x = 4$. Find $y$ when $x = 12$.

$$y = \frac{k}{x},\ k = 6 \times 4 = 24 \quad\Rightarrow\quad y = \frac{24}{12} = 2$$
