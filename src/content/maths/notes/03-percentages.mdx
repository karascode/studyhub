# Percentages

<Key>
"Per cent" means "out of 100". $35\% = \tfrac{35}{100} = 0.35$.
</Key>

## Finding a percentage of an amount

Convert to a decimal and multiply. $18\%$ of $250 = 0.18 \times 250 = 45$.

## Multipliers

A **multiplier** does an increase or decrease in one step.

| Change | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| Increase by 12% | $1.12$ |
| Decrease by 12% | $0.88$ |
| Increase by 5.5% | $1.055$ |

## Percentage change

$$\text{percentage change} = \frac{\text{change}}{\text{original}} \times 100\%$$

## Reverse percentages

If a price *after* a change is known, divide by the multiplier to get the original.

<Mistake>
A jacket costs 68 dollars after a 15% discount. The original is **not** $68 \times 1.15$. The 68 dollars is $85\%$ of the original, so original $= 68 \div 0.85 = 80$ dollars.
</Mistake>

## Compound change

Repeating the same percentage change $n$ times:

$$\text{final} = \text{start} \times \text{multiplier}^{\,n}$$

Money $P$ invested at $r\%$ compound interest for $n$ years: $A = P\left(1 + \dfrac{r}{100}\right)^n$.

<Tip>
On your graphic calculator you can store the multiplier and repeatedly multiply by it, or use the finance app. For "show your working" questions, still write the multiplier method.
</Tip>

## Worked example

2000 dollars is invested at $4\%$ compound interest for 3 years.

$$A = 2000 \times 1.04^3 = 2249.73 \text{ dollars}$$
