# Indices, standard form and surds

<Key>
An **index** (power) tells you how many times to multiply a number by itself: $a^n = a \times a \times \dots \times a$ ($n$ times).
</Key>

## Laws of indices

$$a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n} \qquad \frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n} \qquad (a^m)^n = a^{mn}$$

$$a^0 = 1 \qquad a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n} \qquad (ab)^n = a^n b^n$$

Examples: $2^3 \times 2^4 = 2^7$, $\dfrac{x^6}{x^2} = x^4$, $(3x^2)^3 = 27x^6$, $5^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{25}$.

<Mistake>
$2^3 \times 2^4$ is $2^7$, **not** $4^7$. The base stays the same; only the indices add.
</Mistake>

## Fractional indices

$$a^{1/2} = \sqrt{a} \qquad a^{1/n} = \sqrt[n]{a} \qquad a^{m/n} = \left(\sqrt[n]{a}\right)^m$$

For example $27^{2/3} = \left(\sqrt[3]{27}\right)^2 = 3^2 = 9$ and $16^{-1/2} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{16}} = \dfrac14$.

## Standard form

A number in **standard form** is written $a \times 10^n$ where $1 \le a < 10$ and $n$ is an integer.

- $45\,000 = 4.5 \times 10^4$ and $0.0032 = 3.2 \times 10^{-3}$.
- Large numbers have positive $n$; small numbers have negative $n$.
- **Multiply / divide**: do the numbers, then add / subtract the powers of 10.
- **Add / subtract**: convert to ordinary numbers or the same power of 10 first.

$$(3 \times 10^5) \times (2 \times 10^{-2}) = 6 \times 10^3$$

<Tip>
After a calculation check $a$ is still between 1 and 10. $12 \times 10^3$ must become $1.2 \times 10^4$.
</Tip>

## Surds

A **surd** is an irrational root that cannot be simplified to a whole number, such as $\sqrt2$ or $\sqrt{7}$.

- $\sqrt{a} \times \sqrt{b} = \sqrt{ab}$ and $\sqrt{\dfrac{a}{b}} = \dfrac{\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{b}}$.
- **Simplify** using square factors: $\sqrt{50} = \sqrt{25 \times 2} = 5\sqrt2$.

<Ext>
**Rationalising the denominator**: multiply top and bottom by the surd.
$$\frac{6}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{6\sqrt3}{3} = 2\sqrt3$$
For a denominator like $2+\sqrt3$ multiply by the **conjugate** $2-\sqrt3$, since $(2+\sqrt3)(2-\sqrt3) = 1$.
</Ext>

## Worked example

Write $\dfrac{(6 \times 10^8)}{(2 \times 10^3)}$ in standard form.

$$= \frac{6}{2} \times 10^{8-3} = 3 \times 10^5$$
