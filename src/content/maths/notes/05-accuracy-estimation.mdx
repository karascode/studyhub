# Rounding, estimation and limits of accuracy

<Key>
Round **only at the end** of a calculation. Keep full calculator values in between so that your final answer is accurate.
</Key>

## Decimal places and significant figures

- **Decimal places (dp):** count from the decimal point. $3.4768$ to 2 dp is $3.48$.
- **Significant figures (sf):** count from the **first non-zero digit**. $0.004\,376$ to 2 sf is $0.0044$. $52\,380$ to 3 sf is $52\,400$.
- Look at the next digit: **5 or more rounds up**, 4 or less stays.

In 0607 final answers are usually given to **3 significant figures** unless the question says otherwise. Angles in degrees are to **1 dp**.

<Mistake>
Zeros that hold a place still count when rounding: $5.0$ to 2 sf is $5.0$ (not $5$). Also, $0.0705$ has 3 sf (the zero between 7 and 5 counts).
</Mistake>

## Estimation

Round every number to **1 significant figure** and then calculate.

$$\frac{48.7 \times 3.2}{0.51} \approx \frac{50 \times 3}{0.5} = 300$$

Use estimates to check that a calculator answer is sensible.

## Limits of accuracy

A measurement rounded to a given accuracy lies in a range:

| Given | Lower bound | Upper bound |
|---|---|---|
| 12 cm to the nearest cm | 11.5 | 12.5 |
| 4.7 to 1 dp | 4.65 | 4.75 |
| 300 to 1 sf | 250 | 350 |

Write the range as $11.5 \le x < 12.5$.

### Calculations with bounds

| Calculation | Largest result | Smallest result |
|---|---|---|
| $a + b$ | upper + upper | lower + lower |
| $a - b$ | upper $a$ − lower $b$ | lower $a$ − upper $b$ |
| $a \times b$ | upper × upper | lower × lower |
| $a \div b$ | upper $a$ ÷ lower $b$ | lower $a$ ÷ upper $b$ |

<Tip>
For a division or subtraction, the "bottom" number must use the **opposite** bound to give the extreme result.
</Tip>

## Percentage error

$$\text{percentage error} = \frac{\text{error}}{\text{true value}} \times 100\%$$

## Worked example

A rectangle has length $8.4\ \text{cm}$ and width $5.2\ \text{cm}$, both to 1 dp. Find the upper bound for the area.

Upper bounds are $8.45$ and $5.25$.

$$\text{upper area} = 8.45 \times 5.25 = 44.3625\ \text{cm}^2$$
