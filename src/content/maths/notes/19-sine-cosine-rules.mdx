# Sine rule, cosine rule and area of a triangle

<Key>
For triangles **without** a right angle, use the **sine rule** or **cosine rule**. Label the triangle so that side $a$ is opposite angle $A$, $b$ is opposite $B$ and $c$ is opposite $C$.
</Key>

## Which rule to use?

| You know | You want | Use |
|---|---|---|
| a side and its **opposite** angle, plus one more angle or side | another side or angle | **sine rule** |
| **two sides and the angle between them** | the third side | **cosine rule** |
| **all three sides** | an angle | **cosine rule** |

## The sine rule

$$\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C}$$

For finding an angle, invert it: $\dfrac{\sin A}{a} = \dfrac{\sin B}{b}$.

Example: $A = 40^\circ$, $B = 65^\circ$, $a = 8$. Find $b$.
$$b = \frac{8 \sin 65^\circ}{\sin 40^\circ} = 11.3$$

<Ext>
The sine rule can give **two possible angles** when finding an angle: $\sin\theta = \sin(180^\circ - \theta)$. If the question says "obtuse", choose the larger one.
</Ext>

## The cosine rule

To find a **side**:
$$a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc\cos A$$

To find an **angle**:
$$\cos A = \frac{b^2 + c^2 - a^2}{2bc}$$

Example: $b = 7$, $c = 9$, $A = 50^\circ$.
$$a^2 = 49 + 81 - 126\cos 50^\circ = 130 - 80.99 = 49.0 \quad\Rightarrow\quad a = 7.00$$

## Area of a triangle

$$\text{area} = \tfrac12 ab \sin C$$

where $C$ is the angle **between** sides $a$ and $b$.

## Bearings

A **bearing** is an angle measured **clockwise from north**, written with three digits (e.g. $045^\circ$). Draw a north line at each point and use alternate/co-interior angles.

<Tip>
Check your calculator is in **degrees**, and keep unrounded values until the final answer.
</Tip>

<Mistake>
Using the cosine rule's minus sign wrongly when the angle is obtuse: $\cos$ of an obtuse angle is negative, so the term $-2bc\cos A$ becomes positive.
</Mistake>

## Worked example

Triangle $ABC$ has $a = 6$, $b = 8$ and $C = 70^\circ$. Find its area.

$$\text{area} = \tfrac12 \times 6 \times 8 \times \sin 70^\circ = 22.6\ \text{square units}$$
